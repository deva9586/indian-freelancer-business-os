Kya mujhe GSTIN hona zaroori hai is pack ko use karne ke liye?
Nahi. Agar tum GST-registered ho to invoice sheet CGST/SGST ya IGST ke saath proper tax invoice banati hai. Agar registered nahi ho, to bhi clients, projects, income aur UPI tracking poori tarah kaam karta hai — bas tax wale columns khali rakhna. Registration tumhe kab chahiye, ye apne CA se confirm karo.
